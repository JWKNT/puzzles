Coral (Skyscrapers)

April 7, 2025
Original ↗
SkyscrapersCoral
A coral is to be drawn in the grid: the coral is orthogonally connected, contains no 2x2 area, and does not 'lock in' any cells; that is, all orthogonally connected groups of non-coral cells are orthogonally connected to the boundary.

Numbers outside the grid show the number of separate coral segments visible in that direction. A segment is considered visible if all segments in front of it are of smaller length.
