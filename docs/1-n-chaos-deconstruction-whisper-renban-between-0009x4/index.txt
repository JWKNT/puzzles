11:T505,Place some numbers from 1 to 9 in the grid such that no number repeats in a row or column. All numbers belong to orthgonally connected regions which must be located by the solver. Regions cannot touch each other orthogonally, but they may diagonally. A region of size N contains the numbers 1 through N exactly once.
<br>
<br>
Every cell on a line must contain a number, and each line must obey at least one of the three following properties:
<br>
<br>
1. Adjacent numbers on the line differ by at least 5.
<br>
<br>
2. The numbers on the line form a consecutive sequence and do not repeat. The sequence does not necessarily have to be in order on the line.
<br>
<br>
3. The values along the line lie strictly between the numbers on the ends of the line. A two-long line cannot obey this property.
<br>
<br>
A grey circle contains an odd digit and a grey square contains an even digit.
<br>
<br>
Diagonally connected cells on a line belong to different regions.
