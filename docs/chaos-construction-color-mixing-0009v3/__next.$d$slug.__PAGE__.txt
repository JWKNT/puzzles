4:T569,Place the numbers from 1 to 9 exactly once in every row, column, and region. Each region is orthogonally connected and must be located by the solver.
<br>
<br>
There are three properties: red, yellow, and blue. A colored cell obeys the properties, and only the properties, of the primary colors that compose it. 
<br>
<br>
Red: the number indicates the total count of cells in the same region as itself in the surrounding 3x3 area.
<br>
<br>
Yellow: the number indicates the total count of cells not in the same region as itself in the surrounding 3x3 area.
<br>
<br>
Blue: the number indicates the number of distinct regions in the surrounding 3x3 area.
<hr>
Explicitly:
<br>
<br>
Orange = Red + Yellow
<br>
<br>
Green = Yellow + Blue
<br>
<br>
Purple = Red + Blue
<br>
<br>
White (grey) = Red + Yellow + Blue
