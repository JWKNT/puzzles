Summon
Place the numbers from 1 to 4 in each room exactly once such that no number touches itself, even diagonally.

Connected blocks of numbers in a row or column form numbers by reading left to right or downwards, respectively. Clues outside the grid are equal to the sum of all such numbers within the corresponding row or column.
